## Public Douyin observations

Public Douyin pages observed in August 2026 show several coexisting formats:

- sub-minute highlight / recommendation clips
- roughly 4–8 minute episode or story-line recaps
- 10–30+ minute “watch it all at once” or deep recap videos

The package therefore uses adaptive duration and treats hook, high-light mix, original-audio share and reset interval as a style profile rather than permanent platform rules.

## Platform guidance

Douyin's public community guidance encourages original, high-quality, complete and watchable content, and discourages rough splicing or content with little substantive value.
